# Wearfolio brand

## Identity

- Name: **Wearfolio**
- Tagline: **See more in what you own.**
- Product language: wardrobes contain **pieces**; saved combinations are **fits**.
- Voice: considered, useful, personal and quietly confident.

## Mark

The Wearfolio `W` is built from three overlapping ribbons. They represent individual
pieces becoming a complete fit. Keep the mark clear of hanger, mannequin and shopping
bag imagery.

`wearfolio-mark.svg` is the editable master. Android vector and density-specific
launcher resources live in `app/src/main/res`.

## Core colours

| Name | Hex |

approved single-colour version.
